How I built it

You gave me a lot to work with: your favorites, your Pakistani and Uzbek lists, the Mosaic picks, and the moments you named. I built every part of the day from those lists first and only filled the gaps with my own. On the night, 92 of the 111 songs came from you, and 68 of them were songs I had never played at a wedding before.

About the times

The times come from the log in my DJ software, which stamps a song when it's loaded onto a deck, so they're close, not exact. The ceremony and the speeches don't show up in a song log, so they're marked where they fell, and they're the gray stretches in the strip at the top. Nothing from your do-not-play list was played.
