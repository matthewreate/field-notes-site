    Vendors

    Who made the night work, and where to find them.

    • Planner

      Jason Mitchell Kahn JMK & Co.

      Smooth, competent, best in class. I'd work with him a hundred more times.

    • AV / Production

      Gala Productions James Hallquist, lead

      Five speakers, lights, projector, monitor. Easy going guys, knew their tech, especially my man Samson on the soundboard.

    • Photographer

      Anna Duncan

      Pro, would expect great candid shots.

    • Cocktail bluegrass

      Melody Allegra (Melody Berger)

      Loved the energy.

    • Grazing table

      Michael Hartung

      Was delicious.

    • Late-night deli

      Katz's Delicatessen

      Self-explanatory.

    • Italian

      Manetta's Ristorante Long Island City

      Loved the pesto pasta.

    • Hot dog cart

      Ben's Kosher Delicatessen frankfurter cart

      Didn't get to try it, but it smelled wonderful — the guests loved it once drinks were flowing.

    • Venue

      Sound River Studios

      Clean bathrooms and a real DIY mega-space.
