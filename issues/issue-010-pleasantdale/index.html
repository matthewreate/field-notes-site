    The Full Log

    Every track I cued, in order. 83tracks

    BPM and key for the crate-diggers. Soundcheck and test cues stripped — this is the room as it actually ran.

    Dinner & the Dances Cocktail and dinner ran mellow and patient — Frank Ocean, Solange, Sade — through the formalities: the first dance, both parent dances, a Persian classic for mother and son.
