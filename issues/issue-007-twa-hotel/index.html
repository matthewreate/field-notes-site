    Vendors

    Who made the night work, and where to find them.

    • Venue

      TWA Hotel

      Eero Saarinen’s 1962 terminal. Cherry-red carpet, white Saarinen curves, a hundred suspended ceiling lights. Every angle considered. Danielle Puma ran a tight crew.

    • Photographer

      Caitlin Cooper

      Worked with her before, on a Korean wedding at Brooklyn Winery last year. She and her assistant were great. Familiar team-up made the no-planner situation workable.

    • Videographer

      Amanda Wilder Amanda Wilder Film Co.

      Very professional, sweet, a little shy.

    • Live music

      Joshua Yang Vocals

      Aurora Skies wedding version, live during dinner. Sound check was clean — knew how to work the mic.

    • Officiant

      Ran the ceremony with the bride and groom — no planner, the three of them held it together.
