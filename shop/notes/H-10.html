Michael Jackson — Thriller

Epic, 1982 · near mint · liner notes

0:00

Everybody has a copy of Thriller. That’s the point. It’s the best-selling record ever made, so the question is never whether you want it, it’s which copy you want.

This one is near mint. Which means it sat somewhere quiet, and the record still sounds the way Quincy Jones and Bruce Swedien meant it to. They were obsessive. Every sound on here was put exactly where it is.

What to listen for, if you think you’ve heard it all: “Baby Be Mine”, track two. It never got a single, so nobody plays it, and it’s as good as anything on the record. And on “Beat It”, that’s Eddie Van Halen, who did the solo for free as a favor and didn’t tell his band.

Seven singles off one album went top ten. Nobody has done that since.
