David Bowie — Diamond Dogs

RCA, 1974 · orange label, Dynaflex, Hollywood pressing · liner notes

0:00

1974. Bowie has just killed off Ziggy Stardust, the Spiders are gone, and Mick Ronson is gone. So he plays most of the guitar on this one himself, and you can hear it. It’s rougher. It’s better for it.

He wanted to make a musical out of Orwell’s 1984 and the estate said no way. So this is what happened to the idea: “1984”, “Big Brother”, a ruined city full of half-dog people. And right in the middle of all that, “Rebel Rebel”, one of the greatest riffs of the decade.

The cover is by Guy Peellaert, Bowie as the dog. On most copies the dog’s anatomy got airbrushed before it shipped. Look for yourself.

This is a 1974 original: orange RCA label, the thin floppy Dynaflex vinyl they used then, pressed in Hollywood. The first version of this record that anyone held.
