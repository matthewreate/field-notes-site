Black Sabbath — Black Sabbath

Warner Bros., 1970 · green label original · liner notes

0:00

This is the first one. Four guys from Birmingham, England, recorded this in about two days at the end of 1969, mostly live in the room, and in the process they more or less invented heavy metal. Nobody told them. They thought they were a blues band.

Drop the needle on side one and don’t skip the first thirty seconds. Rain, a church bell in the distance, and then that riff: three notes, the one the church used to call the devil’s interval. That’s the whole genre being born in real time.

This copy is a 1970 American original, the green Warner Brothers label, not the seventies reissue with the palm trees. Somebody named Shirley put her name sticker on the cover back then. I left it. She bought it new, and she kept it fifty years. That’s a good record.
