Cocktail hour is the room’s first hour — the stretch where the music quietly settles what kind of night it’s going to be, before anyone has been asked to do anything.

Brazilian jazz, soul, and boogie, with a turn through bossa and city pop. Collector records, mostly — the kind that carry a room without leaning on anything the guests walked in already knowing.
