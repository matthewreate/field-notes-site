MATTHEW REATE / PRACTICE / VENUES

Practice

DJ sets for restaurants and bars.

Music that fits the room without taking it over.

I DJ about forty-five weddings a year. On the nights between, I play smaller rooms. Restaurants, bars, tasting rooms, patios. Places where the music should feel like it belongs there, not like someone hired a DJ.

What this is

The Setup

  • Sound

    One compact PA — warm, full, loud enough to fill a room without dominating it. If the venue has a house system, I'll plug into that instead. Either way, it sounds like music, not a sound system.

  • Footprint

    I take up about as much space as a two-top. Controller, one speaker, laptop. No lighting rigs, no fog machines. I show up, I set up in ten minutes, I break down in five.

  • Volume

    Background to mid-level. People should be able to talk without shouting. The music adds texture to the room. It doesn't compete with it.

  • Cost to you

    Nothing. I'm not looking for a paycheck. I'm looking for a room where I can play music I care about, build a following, and give people a reason to come back on a specific night.

Sound

What I Play

Everything is curated to the room. I read the energy, the crowd, the time of night. But here's the palette I pull from for a restaurant or bar set:

  • Bossa nova
  • Latin funk
  • Cumbia
  • Soul
  • Afrobeat
  • Rare groove
  • Disco
  • Boogie
  • Jazz
  • Deep house
  • Tropicalia

No top 40. No requests line. No mic. Just music that makes a room feel better than it did before I got there.

I'm not trying to be the show. I'm trying to make the room feel like itself, but better.

Background

Who I Am

I'm Matthew Reate. I DJ weddings full-time through Non-Traditional Wedding DJs and I've been doing this work for years. I collect records, I write about music and rooms, and I take the craft seriously.

Venue sets are a different thing than weddings. No timeline, no toasts, no bouquet toss. Just music and a room. That's the part I love the most, and it's the part I want to do more of.

You can hear some of my sets on the Mixes page, or read about how I think about rooms in the Notes.

Want to try it? Let's do one night and see.
