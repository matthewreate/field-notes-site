DJ sets for restaurants and bars

Records for the room, at a volume people can talk over.

I DJ about forty-five weddings a year. On the nights in between, I play smaller rooms: restaurants, bars, tasting rooms, patios.

What I play

Bossa nova, Latin funk, cumbia, soul, Afrobeat, rare groove, disco, boogie, jazz, deep house, Tropicália. What I pull depends on the room, the crowd and the time of night.

No Top 40, no requests and no mic.

I’m not trying to be the show. I’m trying to make the room feel like itself, but better.

The setup

One compact PA, or I plug into your house system. I take up about as much space as a two-top: a controller, one speaker and a laptop. No lights, no fog machine. Setup takes ten minutes and teardown takes five.

I keep it between background and medium. People should be able to talk without shouting.

Gear: RANE FOUR controller, compact PA or house system, MacBook Pro.
Setup: 10 minutes. One power outlet needed.
Set length: 2 to 4 hours, flexible.
Volume: conversational. You set the ceiling, I stay under it.
Space: corner table, bar end, patio nook. Anywhere a two-top fits.
Based in Morristown, NJ. Available across North Jersey and NYC.

Cost to you

Nothing. I’m not after a paycheck. I want a room where I can play records I care about, build a following, and give people a reason to come back on a certain night.

Venue sets are different from weddings: no timeline, no toasts, no bouquet toss, just music and a room. It’s my favorite part of DJing, and I want to do more of it.

You can hear some of my sets under mixes.
