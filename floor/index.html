How I counted

My DJ software logs every song I play. This page counts my last 50 weddings, August 2025 to now. The lists are DJ Intelligence’s 2025 charts of the songs guests request: a Top 200 plus seventeen genre and decade lists. A song counts as listed when its title and artist match; remixes and covers of a listed song count as listed too. Soundcheck, and anything that played for less than 30 seconds, doesn’t count. The lists show what guests ask for, not what other DJs play.
